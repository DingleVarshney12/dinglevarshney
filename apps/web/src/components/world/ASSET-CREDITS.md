# 3D world assets

- House structure: Kenney Building Kit (CC0), https://kenney.nl/assets/building-kit
- Office furniture and desk props: Low Poly Office & Tech Pack by reodesuxz. Used under the pack's project-use license; source assets remain in the repository root.

The building-kit color atlas served by the web app is recolored to match the room's warm wood, cream, terracotta, and sage palette. The original asset folders are left untouched.
